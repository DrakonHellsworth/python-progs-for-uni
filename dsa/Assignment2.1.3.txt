Q)Why do we need Prefix and Postfix notations?

Prefix and Postfix notations are needed to avoid using parentheses in mathematical expressions. In infix notation, 
we rely on parentheses to decide the order of operations, but in prefix and postfix, the position of operators and operands alone determines the order, 
making the expression much simpler for a computer to process. Prefix notation, also called Polish Notation, 
and Postfix notation, known as Reverse Polish Notation (RPN), are commonly used in computer science. 
They are especially useful in stack-based programming languages and in building algorithms for evaluating 
expressions because they eliminate ambiguity and make computations faster and more efficient.
